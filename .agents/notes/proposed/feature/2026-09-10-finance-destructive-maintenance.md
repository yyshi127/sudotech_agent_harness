# Agent Note: Finance deletion and write-off

Status: proposed

English | [中文](2026-09-10-finance-destructive-maintenance.zh.md)

## Problem

F3B changes existing accounting records. A successful HTTP response, a failed detail lookup or an empty filtered page cannot prove the intended deletion or write-off. Requests must use the vendor fields established for each operation; caller-generated identifiers or undocumented source identity requirements cannot replace fresh target inspection.

## Proposal

Current checkpoint, 2026-09-15: the vendor-provided minimal voucher-deletion request is `accountName` plus `cerInfoList: [{ cerId }]`; appId and flowId are not deletion fields. The product now derives that request from the bound ledger and a fresh exact-voucher inspection without requiring a local creation record. Focused domain tests and the assembled keyless Web refresh/replay pass. A live adapter experiment creates dedicated voucher244499, sends the minimal deletion body once, observes acceptance and two complete absent inventories, and matches the final41-voucher projection to its pre-test baseline. The [receipt](../../../../critique/f3b-lab/receipts/cf12-minimal-voucher-delete-20260915.json) contains no credentials. Browser-manual-origin and broader linked effects remain separately unqualified, so this note stays proposed. The earlier [six-path receipt](../../../../critique/f3b-lab/receipts/f3b-main-app-acceptance-20260914.json) still verifies current-code CF12/CF15/CF22/CF23/CF24/CF25 through the real main application, two independent readbacks and new-session inspection. The [current handoff](../../../../docs/integrations/youcheng-finance-f3b-handoff.md) owns continuation state.

## Alternatives considered

**Resetting a rejected diagnostic intent.** Rejected: a new approval must have its own fixed attempt directory and exclusive intent, retaining the predecessor's ledger, target and definite rejection. Diagnostic02 requires the original141107/reserved201 record; accepted, unknown, wrong-ledger and wrong-target predecessors cannot qualify it. Its approved id-only request returns `帐套名称不能为空`; the freshly read public specification still lists only id. This establishes an undocumented requirement, not its field name or permission to issue further requests. Read-only recovery reports unchanged inventories separately from successful-deletion comparisons. No product payload change is justified by an untested field-name guess.

**Inferring execution eligibility from projected omissions or numeric currency IDs.** Rejected: the raw public ledger response contains no period state, and a cash-account currencyId is not a currency code. Existing settled or unstated journal currencies do not establish a current mapping. The independent lab reads exact existing sources and preserves missing fields rather than modifying accounts to infer status. A rejected deletion may be investigated with a separately authorized fresh attempt, never by deleting or resetting its consumed intent to reuse prior approval.

**Receipt-linked journal verification.** The independent [raw-field inspection](../../../../critique/f3b-lab/receipts/maintenance-relation-id-20260910.json) establishes the relationId field on both approved fixtures. The product verifier preserves the original accepted receipt and requires bounded, counted, unique matching evidence, including cash account, direction, date, amount, currency and original counterparty. Matching only amount or treating receiptId as journalId is rejected. SHA256 fingerprints compare raw and projected business rows across both inventories; this detects observed changes, not concurrent writes outside those observations. Host and shipping-composition tests retain accepted outcomes through successful and failed readback. This does not establish full-ledger completeness, bank movement or permission for further writes.

**Treat creation success or a lookup error as deletion proof.** Rejected because acceptance does not establish absence, and authorization or network failures can hide existing records.

**Retain or generate appId/flowId for deletion.** Rejected because the vendor-confirmed minimal deletion request does not consume those creation fields. Requiring them excludes valid manual vouchers, while generating them adds an unverified identifier.

**Expose a general API caller or implement a browser extension first.** Rejected because either widens authority or delays the six concrete actions without resolving their business validation and recovery requirements.

The independent AR/AP lab creates separate deletion and settlement fixtures through the existing qualified creation adapter, never through a product seeding feature. It records an exclusive batch intent before creating operation records, retains unknown outcomes rather than resending, and compares existing open items, journals and vouchers after each accepted creation. Read-only execution reports existing markers without recreating them. Creation permission is distinct from exact-object destructive permission; fresh fixtures narrow experiments but do not remove unknown vendor linkage or prove historical-record safety.

## Acceptance criteria

Each action needs exact target selection, complete impact preview, enforced consent, configuration and state revalidation, durable cross-session duplicate prevention, bounded single dispatch, honest outcome classification and independent business readback. Unit tests, real assembled application snapshots, approved live fixtures and recovery tests cover their respective layers. The module-level sender and read-only inspection do not alone complete CF12; all six actions remain required for the F3B Goal.

## Risks

The product-wide deletion guard also asks for confirmation before the Finance Host. The assembled test preserves both approvals rather than exempting a tool by name or disguising its destructive purpose. Consolidating them requires retaining exact-version enforcement inside the Host; duplicate confirmation remains an explicit UX gap.

Public APIs do not establish a conditional delete or vendor transaction lock. State may change after the last read, and unknown outcomes must not be resent. Missing original identity, unobserved links and incomplete readback must remain visible limitations. Test creation authority does not authorize deleting arbitrary existing records; actual deletion and write-off need exact-object approval.
